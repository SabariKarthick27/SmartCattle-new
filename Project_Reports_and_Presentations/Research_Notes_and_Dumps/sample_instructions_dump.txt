P359: This chapter reviews existing literature relevant to the project topic. It highlights previous work, methodologies, and findings that inform the current study.
P360: 
P361: The integration of face recognition technology into attendance systems has gained significant attention due to its potential to automate and secure attendance tracking in educational and corporate environments. Traditional methods such as manual roll calls and RFID-based systems are prone to errors, inefficiencies, and manipulation. Face recognition offers a contactless and reliable alternative.
P362: Barla et al. [1] reviewed current methods and challenges in implementing face recognition-based attendance systems. Their study highlights the use of Haarcascade classifiers and NVIDIA Jetson Nano for real-time image processing in classrooms, emphasizing the shift from manual systems to AI-driven solutions.
P363: 
P364: Thakur and Chakre [2] conducted a systematic review focusing on deep learning-based face recognition techniques. They discussed the limitations of RFID systems and proposed CNN-based models for improved accuracy and scalability in attendance tracking.
P365: 
P366: Rudrawar [3] explored various algorithms such as Local Binary Patterns Histograms (LBPH), FaceNet, and YOLO for face detection and recognition. The study emphasized the importance of feature extraction and matching techniques like Euclidean distance and cosine similarity.
P367: 
P368: Falalu et al. [4] examined the challenges of implementing smart attendance systems in real-world scenarios, including lighting conditions and image quality. They also discussed alternative technologies like QR codes and RFID, comparing
P369: 
P370: their effectiveness with face recognition.
P371: 
P372: Singh et al. [5] reviewed multiple face recognition models including Dlib, ResNet, and Haar-Cascade classifiers. Their work focused on the deployment of these models in smart campus environments, highlighting their efficiency and accuracy.
