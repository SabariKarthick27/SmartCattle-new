P025 [List Paragraph]: Provide a general overview of your invention, including specific keywords and phrases pertaining to the invention: [Briefly provide the basic understanding of the invention and also point out specific keywords.]
P027 [Normal]: The present invention relates to a QR-based cattle management and health-monitoring platform for personalized livestock disease detection using a Composite Health Deviation Index (CHDI). The system assigns a low-cost, durable thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) ear tag with a cryptographic 128-bit UUID Quick Response (QR) matrix to each animal, enabling smallholder farmers to retrieve digital medical histories and record daily multi-signal vitals (daily milk yield, feed consumption, rumination duration, core body temperature, and locomotion activity) using any commodity smartphone camera without expensive RFID readers. The computational backplane constructs personalized rolling moving-average baselines and evaluates multivariate anomaly departures across historical signal covariances using regularized Mahalanobis distance. A temporal trajectory tracking module computes 7-day regression slopes to detect subclinical mastitis, lameness, and metabolic disorders up to four days prior to acute symptom onset. The invention incorporates dual-tier Leave-One-Covariate-Out (LOCO) permutation feature explainability, an analytical data-sufficiency gate to avoid premature alerts, and an automated closed-loop clinical triage workflow that dispatches prioritized VetCase tickets and ingests verified veterinary feedback to continuously refine machine learning risk classifiers.

P055 [Heading 1]: Infrastructure-Light, Zero-Cost Optical Identification
P056 [Normal]: Prior systems require expensive RFID readers, antennas, or active neck collars with high battery maintenance costs.
P057 [Normal]: The invention uses a durable, weather-proof TPU ear tag with a cryptographic 128-bit UUID QR code readable by any commodity smartphone browser, eliminating capital hardware costs for smallholder farmers.
P058 [Heading 1]: Adaptive Personalized Baseline vs. Rigid Population Norms
P059 [Normal]: Existing livestock tools apply static, global threshold rules (e.g. fixed milk yield drop), ignoring breed, age, and individual baseline differences.
P060 [Normal]: The invention constructs dynamic rolling moving-average baselines (μ, σ) unique to each animal, isolating true physiological distress from natural individual variance.
P061 [Heading 1]: Multivariate Covariance-Weighted Anomaly Detection (CHDI)
P062 [Normal]: Traditional methods analyze single variables in isolation, missing correlated cross-signal distress patterns.
P063 [Normal]: The invention calculates regularized Mahalanobis distance across the empirical 4x4 covariance matrix of all monitored signals, capturing multidimensional health departures in a normalized 0–100 index.
P064 [Heading 1]: Early Subclinical Disease Onset Detection (4 Days in Advance)
P065 [Normal]: Prior art detects bovine disease only when severe, end-stage clinical symptoms appear, leading to permanent milk yield loss.
P066 [Normal]: The invention performs rolling 7-day least-squares linear slope regression over CHDI trajectories, detecting subtle subclinical incubation up to four days prior to acute clinical collapse.
P067 [Heading 1]: Dual-Tier Explainability and Closed-Loop Veterinary Feedback
P068 [Normal]: Commercial machine-learning livestock tools operate as black boxes without clear clinician explanations or clinical feedback capture.
P069 [Normal]: The invention provides LOCO permutation feature attributions (plain-text farmer advisories and detailed z-score vet tables) and dispatches prioritized VetCase tickets that log verified diagnoses for continuous model retraining.
