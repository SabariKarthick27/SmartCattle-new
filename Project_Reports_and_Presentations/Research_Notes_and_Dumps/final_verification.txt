P025 [List Paragraph]: Provide a general overview of your invention, including specific keywords and phrases pertaining to the invention: [Briefly provide the basic understanding of the invention and also point out specific keywords.]
P027 [Normal]: The present invention relates to an AI-driven examination security system designed to eliminate proxy candidates and ensure real-time identity verification inside physical examination halls. The system utilizes multi-camera video input, deep-learning-based facial recognition, liveness/anti-spoofing detection, and continuous behavioural monitoring to verify that the same registered candidate remains seated throughout the exam. The invention automatically matches live facial data with the pre-registered candidate database and seat-map records, detects impersonation attempts, identifies face-hiding or seat-swapping behaviour, and generates immediate alerts to invigilators through a centralized dashboard. The invention enhances exam integrity by providing full-duration, automated, non-intrusive identity consistency tracking along with secure audit logs and evidence reports.
