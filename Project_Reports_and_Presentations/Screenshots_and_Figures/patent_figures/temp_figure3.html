CALCULATE CATTLE HEALTH DEFICIT INDEX (CHDI)
Measures overall deviation against cow's established baseline.
Generates standardized composite health score from 0 to 100.
HEALTHY BAND (SCORE: 80 – 100)
Status: Low Risk / Optimal Condition
All vitals within normal expected baseline.
Action: Continue routine daily logging.
WATCH BAND (SCORE: 60 – 79)
Status: Moderate Risk / Early Warning
Mild deviations detected in feed or milk yield.
Action: Send advisory notification to farmer.
AT-RISK BAND (SCORE: BELOW 60)
Status: High Risk / Critical Deviation
Significant multi-vital health anomaly.
Action: Auto-dispatch urgent case to veterinarian.
