REGIONAL HEALTH DATA AGGREGATOR
1. Spatial Radius Grouping: Aggregates health reports across nearby farms (within 10–25 km radius).
2. 72-Hour Rolling Window: Tracks synchronized symptoms over consecutive days to detect trends.
3. Cluster Anomaly Index (CAI): Computes regional infection rate = (Total Sick Cows ÷ Regional Population).

ROUTINE FARM MONITORING
Isolated / Non-Contagious Pattern
• Vital deviations are isolated to individual animals on a single farm.
• No evidence of disease transmission across neighboring herds.
• Action: Handled through standard individual veterinary checkup queue.
REGIONAL OUTBREAK ALERT
Emergency Multi-Farm Contagion Warning
• Significant synchronized health decline detected across adjacent farms.
• Early indicator of contagious outbreak (e.g., Foot-and-Mouth or Mastitis).
• Action: Broadcasts urgent emergency alerts to Animal Husbandry Dept & vets.
